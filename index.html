Focused on secure, scalable cloud infrastructure

I am a Cloud Infrastructure & Security professional focused on building and understanding secure, scalable cloud environments. My work and learning span Azure infrastructure, networking, DevOps, Terraform, data platforms, and AI infrastructure.

04

Secure

05

06

Document

Capture architecture, assumptions, and implementation details.
